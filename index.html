CLICK TALK / COMPANY JOURNEY

2019
SUPPLY / FIRST SIGNAL

The first bridge

The journey began with Amazon and 1688, connecting capable Chinese factories with overseas demand. By year-end, the first TikTok short-video matrix was already taking shape.

2021
PLATFORM / CONNECTION

Platform connection

In 2021, TikTok UK platform connections became a new focus. From June through August, Amazon operations continued to scale, deepening the team’s understanding of platform-led cross-border commerce.

2022
LIVE / FIRST SYSTEM

Live commerce begins

Early 2022 began with a Thailand TikTok project and a journey into livestream bases for overseas TikTok work. In the second half of the year, the team pushed UK yoga apparel toward a top-front position, bringing the operating model closer to the customer.

2023
HOST / PERMIT / HANGZHOU

Going live for real

The team joined a Guangzhou TikTok livestream base, was invited into a real-hair broadcast, and secured a livestream permit. Back in Hangzhou, it built the real-hair business from zero. By year-end, two hours could generate around US$1,000, with an hourly average near US$800.

2024
PRODUCT / CATEGORY SHIFT

A new category

When TikTok’s platform mechanics shifted, the team began testing synthetic hair. By the third month, a single host reached about US$150,000 in monthly GMV. A repeatable rhythm between product, host and live operation began to emerge.

2025
MARKET / MANY DIRECTIONS

More markets, more directions

By early 2025, monthly GMV reached about US$500,000 and the team began moving into Los Angeles. New projects grew across yoga apparel, fashion, outdoor products, wigs, 3C, Spanish-language US, bags and wellness. In the second half, Malaysia creator livestreams and Huawei broadcasts with an Olympic champion and a popular singer opened new directions. Across these experiments, the expanding portfolio began reaching roughly US$100,000 a month.

2026
COMPANY / NEXT CHAPTER

Click Talk takes shape

At the beginning of 2026, the company formally began its next chapter and built a complete Hangzhou office. The years of experiments now come together as one Click Talk system: source supply, content, live commerce, overseas markets and brand growth.
